ESP32-C3 Pin Connections:

GPIO 0  -> INMP441 SD
GPIO 1  -> MAX98357A DIN
GPIO 2  -> INMP441 SCK, MAX98357A BCLK
GPIO 3  -> INMP441 WS, MAX98357A LRC
GPIO 7  -> Button B4 (to GND)
GPIO 8  -> OLED SDA
GPIO 9  -> OLED SCL
GPIO 10 -> Button B1 (to GND)
GPIO 20 -> Button B2 (to GND)
GPIO 21 -> Button B3 (to GND)
3V3/MCU -> VDD/3V3 of all modules
GND     -> All grounds
